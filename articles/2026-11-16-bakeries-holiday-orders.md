Bakeries across the area say holiday pre-orders have arrived earlier and in larger numbers than in any previous season.

Owners point to a mix of causes: more people hosting gatherings, a steady interest in locally made food, and an early start to gift shopping.

## Advice for shoppers

1. Order as early as possible, since popular items sell out.
2. Ask about pickup windows before you commit.
3. Confirm any allergy information directly with the shop.
