A local high school robotics team earned a spot in the state finals after finishing near the top of its regional competition.

Team members spent months designing, building and testing their robot, balancing practice with schoolwork. Mentors say the experience teaches teamwork as much as engineering.

The state finals are scheduled for next month. The team is accepting volunteers and small donations to help cover travel costs.
