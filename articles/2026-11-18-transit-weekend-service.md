The regional transit board voted this week to expand weekend bus service, adding more frequent trips on the busiest lines and two new late-night routes.

Board members said weekend ridership has grown steadily and that the added service responds to years of rider requests.

## Key changes

- Buses on major lines every 15 minutes on Saturdays and Sundays
- Two new late-night routes serving the downtown core
- Updated schedules posted at stops and online before the change takes effect

The expansion is expected to begin next spring, pending final approval of the operating budget.
