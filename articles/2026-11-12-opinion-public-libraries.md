Every few years someone announces that libraries are obsolete. Every few years, the lines at the front desk say otherwise.

A library is more than a building full of books. It is a place to apply for a job, to learn a language, to study quietly, or simply to sit somewhere warm without being asked to buy anything.

> A good public space does not ask what you can pay. It asks what you would like to learn.

Funding decisions are made every year, and they are worth paying attention to. Showing up to a budget meeting is a small act that carries real weight.
