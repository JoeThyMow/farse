Harbor District Reopens After Two-Year Renovation

By Staff Reporter  |  November 19, 2026
Filed under: local community

The Harbor District opened its doors again this week after a renovation that stretched across two years, bringing back a public promenade, a restored ferry landing and more than thirty storefronts.

Crowds gathered along the waterfront on opening day, where organizers described the project as a rebuild of the neighborhood's public spaces rather than a simple repaint.

What is new

  • A widened waterfront promenade with seating and shade structures
  • A restored ferry landing with covered waiting areas
  • Ground-floor retail space for local businesses
  • Improved lighting and step-free access throughout

What comes next

A second phase, focused on the east pier, is scheduled to be discussed at an upcoming public meeting. Residents are invited to review the plans and submit comments.
